## Prune 1.0.1

**Updating from 1.0.0:** run `Prune-1.0.1-Setup.exe` over your current Prune. No need to uninstall; your results, settings and backups stay. (Portable: unzip over the old folder.)

### Fixed

- **Chat names:** when you took a screenshot during a call, the call bar at the top ("Name - 4:08") gave the chat its name instead of the chat underneath. The same happened with WhatsApp notification and incoming-call pop-ups. Call bars and pop-ups are now skipped, and the chat list is no longer treated as a chat with someone.
- **Payment apps:** a payment to a shop with a Paytm business account (UPI IDs like `paytm.xxxx@pty`) was labelled as made with Paytm. The app is now read from the screen only.
- **Smoother Review:** the group list on the left no longer recounts thousands of items on every redraw.

### New

- **Know when there's an update (optional):** Prune asks once whether it may check GitHub for new versions. If you say yes, it checks once a day, sending nothing about you or your phone, and Home tells you when a new version is out. If you say no, Prune never goes online.
- **Send feedback** from the home screen, and **Share** your accuracy results straight to a short public form.
- **Clearer phone setup:** exact steps for Samsung (including Auto Blocker), Xiaomi, Pixel, OnePlus/OPPO/realme, Nothing, Motorola and vivo, and a live check that tells you if your cable only charges.
- **A new look:** the dark "Night archive" colours.
