## Prune 1.0.0

**Your phone is hiding gigabytes. Prune finds them, and shows proof for every pick.**

Plug your Android phone into a Windows laptop. Prune reads it, checks every photo, screenshot and file on the laptop, and shows what is safe to remove, each with the reason why. Nothing changes on the phone until you press Clean, and nothing ever leaves your laptop.

### Download

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Prune-1.0.0-Setup.exe` | Installer. Installs for your Windows account only, no admin rights needed. Uninstall from Settings → Apps. |
| `Prune-1.0.0.zip` | Portable. Unzip anywhere and run `Prune.exe`; keep the files together. |

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), an Android phone with USB debugging, and a USB cable that carries data. The setup screen walks you through USB debugging for your phone's brand. See the [user guide](https://github.com/Roushan-2104/Prune-CleanUp/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md).

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because Prune isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You can check the files against these SHA-256 checksums first:


In PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Prune-1.0.0-Setup.exe`

### What's in 1.0

- **Finds what your phone hides:** files Android leaves out of its own list, including WhatsApp's copies of everything you've sent.
- **Proof for every pick:** pictures, PDF pages, app icons and playable videos; "How we know" facts; duplicates side by side with the copy that stays.
- **Understands screenshots:** chats grouped by person and app, payments recognised by their success message and proof, other screens grouped by app or site.
- **Safe cleaning:** picks go to the phone's Trash for 30 days, hidden files are backed up to your laptop first, every file is re-checked before removal, and Undo works, even after a pulled cable.
- **Private:** no account, no upload, no analytics, no internet use. Screenshot text is encrypted with your Windows account.
- **Fast:** about 16,000 photos checked in under 20 minutes with the laptop plugged in.

### Known limits

Android only. Photos in Google Photos' cloud aren't checked, only what is on the phone. HEIC photos need Microsoft's HEIF/HEVC extensions.
