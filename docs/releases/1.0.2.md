## Prune 1.0.2

**Updating:** run `Prune-1.0.2-Setup.exe` over your current Prune. No need to uninstall; your results, settings and backups stay. (Portable: unzip over the old folder.)

### New

- **Cancel a clean-up:** the clean-up window now has a Cancel button. Prune stops after the step it is on, never in the middle of a file, and puts back everything it already cleaned, so the phone ends up as it was. Cancel while backing up changes nothing on the phone.
- **Much faster cleaning and Undo:** files are now changed several at a time, with the same checks before and after every change. On our test phone, cleaning is about 7 times faster and Undo about 5 times faster.

### Fixed

- **Undo keeps dates:** files put back from the laptop backup keep their old date, so they return to their place in your gallery instead of showing up as new.
- **Chat names:** Prune now reads where each line sits on the screenshot. The chat's name is the line in the header next to the profile picture, so a name the app cut short (Instagram), a call bar, or a notification over the chat no longer gives the wrong name. Contact lists are no longer treated as chats. Screenshots are read once more on the next connection to learn these positions.
- **Photo check:** deleting a photo on the phone while Prune was checking photos stopped the check with "The phone was disconnected". Now only that photo is skipped.
- **Smoother Home screen** while photos are being checked.
- **Tidier look:** calmer type weights, even spacing, and both lists on Home lined up.
